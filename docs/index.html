SayTV

A voice remote for TVs that run Android TV or Google TV.

Tap the microphone and say "open Netflix", "volume 30", "mute" or "pause". Your TV does it. You can also type a command, or use the full on-screen remote with your own list of streaming apps.

No account, no ads, no analytics. SayTV talks to your TV directly over your home Wi-Fi.

Support

Privacy

Read the SayTV Privacy Policy. In short: the app collects nothing and has no servers of ours.

Good to know

  • Works with TVs and streaming boxes that run Android TV or Google TV. It does not work with Fire TV, Roku, Samsung, LG or Apple TV.
  • Your phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Pairing takes one six-character code shown on the TV.
  • A TV in standby usually cannot be switched on over Wi-Fi: use the TV's own remote for that.
