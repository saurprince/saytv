SayTV Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Applies to the SayTV app for Android and iPhone.

In short. SayTV has no accounts and no servers of ours. It does not collect your personal data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools. It talks to your TV over your home Wi-Fi. If you use the microphone button, your phone's own speech recognition turns your voice into text, and that service may send the audio to Apple or Google (see section 4).

1. Who we are

SayTV is published by VISHWAVERSE LLP, Pune, Maharashtra, India ("we", "us"). This policy covers the SayTV app only. It does not cover the VisswaVerse website or the VisswaVerse app, which have their own policy at visswaverse.com/privacy.

2. What the app keeps on your phone

Everything below stays on your phone. We never receive it.

Choose Forget this TV in the app menu to delete the pairing key, the TV's address and its name. Uninstalling the app on Android removes everything. On iPhone, Keychain items can remain after an app is deleted, so use Forget this TV before you delete the app.

3. Your TV and your network

The app connects to your TV directly, over your home network, using an encrypted connection (TLS). To find your TV it can look for devices on your local network that announce an Android TV remote service, or try the addresses on your local subnet. It only keeps a device if you choose to pair with it. Nothing about your TV or your network is sent to us.

The commands you send (volume, opening an app, navigation) are handled by software on your TV, which is provided by your TV's maker and Google under their own terms.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you tap the microphone button in the app. The audio goes to your phone's built-in speech recognition (Apple's on iPhone; Google's on Android). Depending on your phone and settings, that service may process the audio on the phone or send it to Apple or Google, under their privacy policies. The app receives only the resulting text. We never receive your audio or your text, and the app does not record or store it. The words are matched to a command on your phone.

You can use the app without the microphone by typing a command or using the buttons.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone and speech recognition: for the microphone button (section 4).
  • Local network (iPhone): to find and talk to your TV on your Wi-Fi.
  • Network access (Android): to talk to your TV on your Wi-Fi.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share personal data. We hold none.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking.
  • The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

Apple and Google may give us aggregated statistics about the app, such as installs and crash counts, from people who allowed that in their phone settings. These do not identify you.

7. Children

SayTV is not directed at children, and it does not collect anything from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights and complaints

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can still write to us with any question or request. In line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, our Grievance Officer will acknowledge a complaint within 24 hours and resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
